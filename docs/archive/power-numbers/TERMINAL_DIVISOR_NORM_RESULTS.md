# Terminal divisor-norm research — condensed summary

## Key findings

- Terminal local PASS can be expressed as a relative ideal-norm condition for both Fermat and Mersenne towers.
- The natural non-cancelling aggregate is a regular-Artin/Euler-style coefficient, but fixed aggregate Artin characters are structurally blind to the required local support pattern.
- Fixed linear Galois/Fitting summaries are also blind on the adversarial controls.
- Raw powering cannot be renamed as binary composition: a compact descriptor still has to pay for specialization unless a smaller closed quotient is proved.
- The terminal theorem is a **local support decision**, not a missing global scalar norm identity.

## Rejected shortcuts

- Aggregate terminal Artin character as an exact classifier.
- Fixed linear Galois/Fitting data.
- Hiding the classical nonlinear chain behind an unevaluated powered descriptor.

## Frontier

- Find a candidate-derived locality-preserving support packet or an exact natural GCD split that avoids factor/CRT reconstruction and does not exceed the Pépin/LL ceiling.
