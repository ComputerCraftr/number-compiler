# Special germ descent/orientation research — condensed summary

## Key findings

- Polynomial perturbation gives an exact quotient map but does not itself reveal the missing support/orientation observer.
- The local descent orientation is naturally a ring-valued/CRT idempotent object, not always one global Boolean bit.
- Finite character vectors cannot generally replace that orientation without losing mixed-support cases.
- Direct APR/cyclotomic/Frobenius specializations collapse to the same base-ring section on the tested construction and therefore do not create a new injector.
- Sparse products, digits, and carries supply useful aggregate constraints but do not by themselves decide local support.
- The production-safe consequence is to keep unresolved support guarded and specialize only when a valid constraint/certificate is available.

## Frontier

- Construct the terminal support/orientation packet from the unfactored candidate without factor/CRT truth data.
- Any proposed scalar replacement must be tested against mixed CRT-idempotent counterexamples.
- Output-relative constraints may eliminate impossible sheets, but raw source digits/length alone cannot distinguish prime from composite versions of the same integer.
