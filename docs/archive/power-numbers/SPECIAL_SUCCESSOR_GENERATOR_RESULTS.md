# Special-number group-depth / successor audit — condensed summary

## Key findings

- On independently verified prime Fermat/Mersenne controls, minimized Moore behavior matches nested successor-image depth exactly.
- Composite M11 is the required guard: its image-depth partition is **not** its Moore behavior, so prime-domain formulas cannot be used as primality assumptions.
- Cheap Jacobi/residue features provide sound one-way exclusions but do not identify all middle 2-primary depth layers.
- Static cross-base/Jacobi feature sets nearly reconstruct bounded carriers; they are not acceptable compact injectors.
- Alternate-radix structured automata share bounded tables but still grow with carrier size.
- Middle image predicates are dense in ordinary Boolean coordinates; a raw Boolean-vector morphism does not expose the missing state.
- The actual prime-domain quotient is a pure 2-primary group-depth coordinate after removing the known radix subgroup / split core.
- Once that coordinate is known, temporal powering is logarithmic through the existing valuation-sensitivity/binary morphism machinery.
- Factor-backed CRT audits prove what the compact local shape must represent, but factors are research truth data only.
- Carry/radix histories are theorem-discovery sensors; exact minimization quotients their phase away on prime controls.
- Endpoint-only block sampling is unsound because it loses the within-block remainder/output trace.
- Existing primary/cyclotomic word features do not yet construct the compact purity/depth state factor-free.
- The historical `O(sqrt(h))` factor-window short-circuit is an exact composite witness only; exhaustion remains `UNKNOWN` and is not a purity proof.

## Current frontier

- Derive the compact 2-primary purity/depth interface directly from the unfactored special form.
- Do not reintroduce carry history, factor identity, CRT reconstruction, or an endpoint-only sampled quotient as semantic state.
- Production theorem resolution remains lazy and uses the classical Pépin/LL ceiling when the compact constraint state cannot resolve the request.
