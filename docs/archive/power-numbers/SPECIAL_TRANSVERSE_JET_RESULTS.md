# Transverse jet / arithmetic holonomy research — condensed summary

## Closed results

- The special-number circle defect has an exact transverse-jet interpretation.
- Admissible special factors lie on explicit arithmetic progressions, giving exact interval holonomy and left/right forcing formulas.
- The natural GCD defect detects bounded composite controls such as F5/M11/M29.
- Balanced interval-product semantics and Fermat sparse binomial normal forms are exact.

## What did not close

- Equal-span dyadic siblings do not follow from a bounded exact state currently known.
- Binary interval descriptors compress construction history but do not remove the growing product/evaluation work.
- Writing the block as a rising factorial or Gamma value does not make it cheap.

## Frontier

- Evaluate a progression/rising-factorial holonomy block from the compact special candidate without enumerating its boundary factors, then feed the resulting support packet into the existing constrained special germ.
- Any sublinear/factorial/multipoint replacement must charge polynomial state and arithmetic work explicitly.
