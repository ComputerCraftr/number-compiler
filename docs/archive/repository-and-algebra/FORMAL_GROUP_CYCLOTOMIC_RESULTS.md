# Formal-group / cyclotomic-conjugacy research — condensed summary

## Key findings

- A global additive-to-multiplicative conjugacy cannot exist uniformly across the hidden finite-field axes.
- Finite-window interpolation does not repair the missing characteristic-dependent information.
- Denominator-free exponential formulas capture only tangent information; exact formal exp/log recovery reaches denominator order tied to the hidden factor characteristic.
- In characteristic `q`, the native additive formal group can collapse once `q` is already known—so this is post-factor simplification, not factor-free acquisition.
- Frobenius forcing yields divisibility constraints but not a free aggregate over the entire factor window.
- Fermat admits a real one-query criterion from this machinery, but its work is worse than Pépin on the intended scale.
- Simple Gaussian/Pell norm modules are splitting-blind on the controls.

## Frontier

- Do not pursue a universal formal-group conjugacy as the missing injector.
- Any useful cyclotomic/formal-group coordinate must be candidate-derived without first learning the hidden characteristic and must beat the existing classical ceiling in charged work.
