# Tropical ghost-flux research — condensed summary

## Closed results

- GCD/divisor support admits an exact factor-free tropical valuation/divisor semiring for multiplication.
- Saturated multiplication/min/max compose with constant-size divisor state once the relevant axes are known.
- The decisive ghost appears at an equal-valuation/tropical tie; ordinary min-plus loses the phase needed to distinguish cancellation.
- Divisor-only state is not closed under ring addition.
- Enriching with enough leading phase information eventually reconstructs the ring residue, defeating the intended compression.
- Given hidden factor axes, their periodic tropical dynamics are extremely small; acquiring those axes is the hard step.
- Saturation destroys reversible left/right transport in the naive enriched model.

## Rejected shortcuts

- Plain min-plus source detection.
- A single supertropical tie marker.
- Universal finite phase enrichment that stays smaller than the ring state.
- Treating constant-size post-factor dynamics as a factor-free constructor.

## Frontier

- The useful tropical kernel must be **accumulated from candidate-derived constraints** or supplied by an exact cheaper observer; it must not be obtained by enumerating factor slots.
- Production special-number resolution no longer uses the exponential tropical scan; classical Pépin/LL is the hard ceiling when lazy constraints do not decide the requested output.
