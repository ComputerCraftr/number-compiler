# NC15 output morphisms and Boolean counter integration

Period: 2026-10-11 through 2026-10-17 (Sunday–Saturday).

[Week index](README.md)

## Objective

Repair final/entry bank coordinates and connect source-authenticated spatial
output morphisms and scalar residue observations to the existing CLI owners.
Integrate the finite-precision Boolean-counter source and signed Fermat fold
without treating partial source coverage as a primality certificate.

## Changes

The 2rev10 pointed producer converts final pair coordinates back through the
admitted left-birth count before consulting a protected source bank. Its finite
window and periodic morphism consumers use the same source prefix. The periodic
producer checks both moving halos, including the additional frame needed by
the conditional borrow observer. Orbit scratch storage uses the existing ledger.

Reverse/add exposes a source-owned spatial morphism over the admitted coefficient
period and border-growth control. Coefficient emission, five-band counts and
Walsh projection share the powered bank. The independent mod-99 observer uses
the bounded six-step collapse and modular powering; it does not publish a Q19
endpoint merely to answer a residue request.

Spatial windows are retained by the Q19 owner after their complete interval
guard passes. Copying or resealing a window cannot borrow another window's
authority, and failed publication rolls back its registry entry. Requested
band channels share one coupled-frame projection.

The registry owns `temporal.residue99`, `spatial.bank-morphism` and the 2rev10
`spatial.bank-window` demand. Summary and metrics requests do not activate them.
The residue clock class is calculated directly; production does not run an
exhaustive Moore minimizer on every observation.

Fermat's finite binary source comes from the normalized-unit binomial expansion.
The two-carry signed-fold action supports chronological composition and repeated
operand words. A complete-source certificate is consumed for F1–F3; incomplete
windows cannot authorize a positive or negative primality verdict. Shared source
and fold results are retained within the demanded packet instead of recomputed
for each diagnostic projection.

Counter provenance binds the exact issued record and typed payload snapshot;
copying its seal, swapping an equal fold object or replacing an integer by a
Boolean alias does not authorize another certificate. Validation reads that
provenance without replaying the binomial source. The raw-field `_issue` factory
was removed: the checked source demand seals its result only after computing
the source, carry masks and fold action.

## Established results

The expanded focused runtime selection passes 494 tests, including final/entry
bank coordinates, exact independent outputs, lazy CLI demands, forged and stale
authority rejection, and scratch-reservation cleanup. Controlled reverse banks
retain each discovered successor for linking instead of recomputing the complete
growth-control orbit a second time. The regression counts one advance per
discovered state and control symbol.

The 2rev10 executable arithmetic, reverse/add residue and growth-admission
sessions pass. The growth proof applies the chronological composition theorem
directly instead of simplifying the same five-step recurrence again. The Fermat
session also passes, including the six bounded binomial-source instances and
their F1–F3 primality bridge. These are kernel builds, not merely source or
proof-inventory checks.

## Rejected approaches

Equal mod-99 residues do not authorize replacing a digit word, growth itinerary
or source guard. A finite source bank is not a full-root temporal quotient.
The periodic emitter must not claim global future Moore minimality from an
observed lasso. Public output coordinates must not be passed directly to an
entry-relative bank.

For Fermat, low binary windows omit high-bit signed-fold corrections at larger
indices. The downloaded audit explicitly exhibits the missing F4 correction.
The new bounded method is not labeled scalable, and failure of a sufficient
prime condition is not promoted to a composite verdict. No candidate-modulus
Pépin or Lucas–Lehmer recurrence was introduced.

The signed-carry composition theorem and normalized-unit-to-primality theorem
do not by themselves refine the Python binomial source loop. That refinement
must establish its modular coefficient invariant and vanishing tail for
unrestricted levels. The new executable producer model instead checks the six
actual F1–F3 complete-window instances, including the Python stopping guards
and nonnegative exponents. Bounded facts must not be relabeled as a universal
producer theorem.

The producer proof publishes each closed accumulator state once. Each proposed
odd inverse is checked by its range and modular product; no ML-computed value is
accepted as a premise. Index equalities are normalized to the consumer's numeral
form before reuse. The evaluator rejects an unconsumed source state or a
non-numeric successor instead of falling back to recursive reevaluation.
