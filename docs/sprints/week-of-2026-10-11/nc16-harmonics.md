# NC16 source-bound harmonic rings and open temporal banks

Period: 2026-10-11 through 2026-10-17 (Sunday–Saturday).

[Week index](README.md)

## Objective

Connect the NC16 finite harmonic rings and admitted border macros to existing
family producers. Share refusal-preserving finite actions without promoting
an observed input word into an unconditional future certificate.

## Changes

Collatz selects source-certified constant-tail and short changing-tail 6305
returns before individual return discovery, reuses their powered affine action
at publication, and admits scratch work before constructing coefficients.
The exact dyadic guard retains the final endpoint parity bit. Demanded prime,
radix and position projections remain separate from ordinary execution.

The 2rev10 source adapter publishes a finite open inlet program and applies a
Moore-minimized conditional action to its actual certified frames. Reverse/add
publishes bounded inward carry constraints and a finite harmonic ring from its
live Q19 owner. The CLI exposes both through `temporal.open-ring`. Reverse
publications retain exact-object snapshots in ledger-owned storage; a copied
issuer marker does not authorize altered lanes or a foreign owner.

Mahler odd-seam arithmetic separates its ordered ledger delta from fractional
positivity. Its demanded ring consumes admitted Antihydra source coordinates.
All conditional ring count actions use shared chronological composition and
binary powering with explicit refusal, local graph identity and allocation
bounds. No archive-global memo is introduced.

Signed tripling blocks retain the exact weighted source or an unresolved
symbolic middle. Known-block carry maps use three exact endpoint shifts rather
than replaying every input digit. Decimal cyclotomic order has one arithmetic
owner. Signed position factors reduce absolute coordinates modulo twice the
fold width before constructing a bounded shift. Unknown-middle carry relations
use their checked repeating orbit instead of powering once per exponent bit.
A complete signed fold still requires complete source coverage.

quotient-behavior assertions. Independent production odd-event checks replace

## Directed-width and birth-layer follow-on

The October 16 follow-on ports the source-authenticated paired growth tape and
signed diagonal masks. Reverse width powering uses a bounded sparse min/max
action over the actual finite inward carry alternatives, with aggregate scratch
reservations and separately measured state, edge and composition work. The
symmetric filter now remembers every earlier non-EQ pair; a later EQ pair cannot
restore an already excluded center dependency. The K=2 regression is exact at
14 steps and correctly reports a range at step 15.

Directed comparisons use the existing Q19 issuance registry. Their first
publication constructs the masks and action; subsequent demands validate the
retained source and snapshot without rebuilding either. Copies and mutated
bilateral metadata do not inherit issuance authority. Empty internal domains
are rejected rather than letting an outer reset fabricate a realizable path.

The new `2rev10 --info temporal.birth-layers` demand advances canonical moving
boundary strips from two inward ports per F tick. It shares the existing source
owner and independently checks one powered endpoint grammar. The K+1-to-K
projection supplies outer-layer inlets, and full-state return models compute
periods rather than assuming a multiplier of two or three. At the reported
144-F-step horizon, seed 196 has seventh-layer period 24 while seed 1 has 48.
Finite source evidence never authorizes an indefinite inlet sequence. Ordinary
metrics and digit requests do not construct this observer.
The source observer reads two inward point cones per F tick; it does not claim
that this finite discovery cost is logarithmic. Repeated conditional actions
are powered only after their complete state and inlet motif have been checked.

Mahler's changing-exponent compiler reuses one compiled chronological motif,
retains safe partial prefixes of a failing motif, and separates integer ledger
safety from fractional positivity. Demanded q-harmonics handle even radices
without division by two. Open orbit prefixes report an unknown period. Public
source records retain exact guards; finite harmonic equality is not authority.
Odd-to-odd actions are issued by checked identity, run and chronological
composition constructors. Direct construction cannot supply invented ledger
counts or prefix minima. Issuance snapshots copy typed scalar guard and safety
fields, so copying a seal, changing a nested coefficient or replacing zero with

Signed positional counts have one family-independent carry owner. Source

## Guarded odd-macro production integration

For a centered odd source D, the executor selects a = v2(3D+1), consumes
`1,0^(a-1)` in centered coordinates, and retains extent a, one odd event,
ledger change 3-a and the exact prefix minimum. The h-coordinate word is the
complement `0,1^(a-1)`. Arithmetic continues after a negative ledger minimum;

repeat capacity before constructing the single full action. The production

implementation scanned an arbitrarily long suffix and failed the existing
1,024/4,096-return operand-work envelopes; that implementation was replaced,
and those unchanged assertions now pass. Long uncovered suffixes retain the
balanced provider. This is a useful conditional contraction, not a general
logarithmic cold-discovery bound.

## Remaining work

The checked proof targets cover shared refusal-preserving composition, finite supplied-inlet trace
composition, signed weighted tripling, source-selected fixed-word Collatz
arithmetic and Mahler seam/ledger transport. The reverse constraint theory proves
sound support filtering and finite local candidate enumeration. Its model does not yet refine

obligations, and arc consistency does not establish global realizability.

repeating future inlets. Fixed-word Collatz powering does not establish the
changing-tail motif selector.
