# NC14 source-bound radix and carry integration

Period: 2026-10-11 through 2026-10-17 (Sunday–Saturday).

[Week index](README.md)

## Objective

Integrate the downloaded NC14 source/radix patches through existing family,
HashLife and CLI owners; preserve exact admission and unresolved proof obligations.

## Changes

The NC14 downloaded patches were selectively integrated into the existing owners.
Shared arithmetic now owns finite three-power radix sources, Walsh/H5 operations,
and non-coprime affine congruence pullbacks. Collatz selects candidates from joint
binary/F5 source data and retains exact admission; Mahler's explicit CLI detail
uses its admitted source action. Multiple carry/borrow masks share one source tape.
Ordinary metrics and value requests do not activate these optional projections.

Reverse/add's source cochain is evaluated on the authenticated Q19 grammar. The
2rev10 period-two output bank has a production point-demand fast path and explicit
`temporal.radix-walsh` / `arithmetic.radix25-shift` consumers. The short-source
binary shift now increments its own counter instead of claiming a transducer
application. Longer words keep their compressed transducer path.

Adversarial review added source/root/generation authentication to the protected
2rev10 packets and separated left/right birth bits when both borders grow. The
Collatz port wrapper now rejects inconsistent odd-event counts and externally
supplied quadratic clocks; checked producers seal all clock moments and validate
that seal before application. A warm checked composition does not recompute the
power of three solely to validate its event count.

Fermat radix projection derives consecutive levels from one finite source residue.
The joint CRT, normalized-unit and Walsh consumers share that source authority;
terminal observations stay lazy. Finite CRT agreement and a supplied terminal halo
are not a proof of primality. Unknown prime checks remain unknown, and no candidate
Pépin or Lucas–Lehmer loop was introduced.

## Established results

Exact-output, source-sharing, phase, authority and demanded-observation tests
cover the migrated constructors. The added local arithmetic theorems pass the
Isabelle kernel. Whole-tree results and remaining release holds are separated below.

## Rejected approaches

A finite radix, Walsh or CRT observation cannot replace the exact parity cylinder
or decide an unproved prime bit. The audit rejects aggregate two-border growth
as a Boolean Walsh lane, exact mod-160 source labels as universal semantic IDs,
and accepting a frozen packet as proof of source issuance.
