## Shared actions and runtime ownership

The week established bounded interfaces, canonical owner reuse, explicit carry
domains, typed port-valued actions, coordinate morphisms, clock normalization,
and demand-specific consumers. Repository and unit cleanup removed obsolete
runtime owners and added refusal paths. These changes reduced duplicate work but
did not prove that supplied summary words can be discovered within a compact
bound.

HashLife and quotient work remained subject to the same acceptance rule:
composition and cache reuse are insufficient without an exact observer quotient,
source coverage, and sublinear construction/demand evidence.

## Reverse/add

Exact pair residues, threshold carry cocycles, contextual residuals, reflected
views, growth coboundaries, and source publisher boundaries were connected and
checked on their stated domains. The work separated spatial carry composition
from temporal publication and identified the complete interface and source
occurrence as necessary state.

The generic powered route remained blocked. Publisher and residual theorems did
not construct arbitrary-duration source packets, and removal of classical or
causal paths did not create the replacement.

## Collatz

Directional carry/output views, Hensel cylinders, source anchors, physical and
event clocks, and geometry-derived ports were connected. Equal normalization
with different support offsets established that endpoint/weight is too small a
quotient. Finite physical/event refinement remained distinct from any claim
about the Collatz conjecture.

## Primes and Goldbach

The deterministic prime-wheel owner, prefix rank/select, and finite progression
counts gained production and proof connections. Their guarantees applied to
admitted finite prefixes and ranked windows. Universal Goldbach multiplicity and
a live paired-residue prover were not established.

## Antihydra and Mahler

Finite arithmetic execution, retained support projections, literal startup,
macro-word laws, and guarded certificate checks were integrated for the pinned
machine. The source-7 all-time barrier was not constructed; the checked barrier
theorem remained conditional on a supplied valid certificate.

## Fermat and Mersenne

Shared square actions, clock/deck observations, primary carry jets, factor
progressions, and conditional terminal bridges were checked. Audits rejected
zero-key and supplied-root shortcuts. The candidate-derived compact
unit/annihilator evaluator remained absent, so unsupported primality requests
continued to return unknown.

## Validation interpretation

Individual packets recorded focused passes, failures, timings, and fingerprints.
Those measurements apply only to their recorded source. By the end of the week,
adversarial review still found open powered-construction, source-global
Antihydra, universal Goldbach, and special-number terminal gates. No component
pass was promoted to an all-family release result.
