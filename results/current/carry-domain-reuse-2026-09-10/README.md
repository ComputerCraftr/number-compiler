## Implemented work

- A one-piece causal boundary delegates directly to its canonical parent output
  or reverse summary. The old two full interval scans computed exits that the
  sole child did not consume. Multi-piece midpoint equations remain unchanged.
- Primitive-pair interface traversal retains only bounded iterative frames and
  values. It computes child contexts once, reuses published child interfaces,
  and reserves workspace before allocation. Independent inventories verify its
  owners, capacity and live handles.
- Region authority uses the live application registry rather than scanning
  every execution root. The preceding application cleanup, bounded pin lookup,
  parent-first point path, and reverse-summary memo remain in place.
- Eligible Collatz radices use two Boolean carry wires and a constant-size
  five-action nonempty semigroup. Empty intervals retain their separate
  identity. This improves live carry metadata; it is not a claim that binary
  physical temporal execution now uses a new future-behavior quotient.
- Two distinct-source reverse transport/birth cancellation witnesses share
  exact successor TileIDs and subsequent powered results. Warm consumer tests
  poison descendant construction.
- The unused finite-tile proof executor moved out of the production package,
  without a forwarding facade. A dead zero-return reporting helper was removed.

dependencies without exact construction. Primitive carry summaries and bounded
Frobenius projections are not interchangeable with arbitrary future-output
behavior. The local growth and forcing observations are implemented; the open
issue is reusable composed-consumer execution and its measured cost.

and the remaining exhaustive release gates stay open. Tile interning hits,
