## Implemented

- Added scoped future-behavior profile and class identities inside `DiamondQuotientArena`. The implemented certificate computes a genuine greatest fixed point for uniform blank intervals under repeated homogeneous primitive actions, quantifying over every right state and requiring certified safe left states. It retains no occurrence tiles or source callbacks.
- Added sealed behavior-classification bindings to the existing completed-result memo. Warm hits validate detached publication metadata and live scoped handles without repeating proof evaluation. Failed cold publication rolls back newly admitted semantic records and result bindings; consumed IDs are not reused. Charged diagnostic miss evidence is retained separately from semantic/result publication.
- Integrated this certified output dependency into the causal scheduler. It can reuse an already canonical blank tile without emitting its future boundary. It does not manufacture the producer's outgoing ports or cocycles; ordinary result tasks remain responsible for those observations.
- Persisted canonical clipped-region normalization under the same execution-memo owner, including application leases and reverse indexes. Repeated observations can reuse the region with clipping, mirroring, concatenation, and joins poisoned. Caller-owned keys and returned views are detached to prevent mutation from corrupting retained keys.
- Added immutable primitive symmetry-certificate admission. Unsupported transformations reject. This does not yet provide symmetry-normalized complete execution requests, and it is not full D4.
- Added separate behavior, region, interning, cache-level, eviction, retirement, and unavailable-recreation counters. Diagnostic histograms are bounded and charged. A leaf interning hit is not reported as a powered-result hit.
- Repaired read-only reporting after Collatz query-cache eviction, typed histogram payload validation, and mixed-snapshot detection. Reporting now rejects store or counter mutation during projection.
- Added profile-enabled/profile-disabled measurements through the same tile scheduler. The benchmark reports an explicitly incomplete instrumented operation vector, rather than inventing a total-work saving or asymptotic conclusion.

## Scope of the behavior certificate

The new class proves the output of **inactive uniform blank regions** under its admitted profile. It is not a general Myhill–Nerode quotient of active reverse-add or Collatz interiors. In particular, it does not yet establish useful same-geometry aliases between different active exact contents.

The retained distinction is important: exact tile equality, bounded transfer equivalence, and future behavior under a consumer profile remain separate. The equal-carry/different-output counterexamples remain valid. A classification hit is not represented as a completed result with fabricated boundary or weight data.

## Remaining execution work

The primitive-pair fused transfer is still only the base case. For P⁴ and P⁸, the producer and consumer may both be composed actions, and their causal contexts include exact boundary operands. The missing implementation is the balanced compiler that derives the fixed consumer-profile transition from child behaviors and both midpoint transfers, without emitting the producer boundary first. Existing decision-DAG storage does not implement that compilation by itself.

The public reverse builder still installs a temporal evaluator. `ReverseRecursiveImageRuntime.point_bound()` and `width_bound()` still reach higher-semantic evaluation. The ten original absorption tests intentionally expose this route. Collatz, accelerated observations, radix, and compatibility migration are also unfinished.

Directly routing public queries to the current exact causal fallback would preserve correctness but worsen the measured performance. It has therefore not been presented as a completed production cutover. No new global trajectory theorem or perfect fingerprint is being asserted as a prerequisite; the remaining work is executable composite classification, reuse, and integration.

Complete-request mirror/conjugacy normalization, useful active behavioral transitions, comprehensive dependency coverage traces, and exact GC recreation attribution are still incomplete. Unknown attribution is not reported as zero or as proven recreation.
