## Scope of the behavior certificate

The new class proves the output of **inactive uniform blank regions** under its admitted profile. It is not a general Myhill–Nerode quotient of active reverse-add or Collatz interiors. In particular, it does not yet establish useful same-geometry aliases between different active exact contents.

The retained distinction is important: exact tile equality, bounded transfer equivalence, and future behavior under a consumer profile remain separate. The equal-carry/different-output counterexamples remain valid. A classification hit is not represented as a completed result with fabricated boundary or weight data.

## Remaining execution work

The primitive-pair fused transfer is still only the base case. For P⁴ and P⁸, the producer and consumer may both be composed actions, and their causal contexts include exact boundary operands. The missing implementation is the balanced compiler that derives the fixed consumer-profile transition from child behaviors and both midpoint transfers, without emitting the producer boundary first. Existing decision-DAG storage does not implement that compilation by itself.

The public reverse builder still installs a temporal evaluator. `ReverseRecursiveImageRuntime.point_bound()` and `width_bound()` still reach higher-semantic evaluation. The ten original absorption tests intentionally expose this route. Collatz, accelerated observations, radix, and compatibility migration are also unfinished.

Directly routing public queries to the current exact causal fallback would preserve correctness but worsen the measured performance. It has therefore not been presented as a completed production cutover. No new global trajectory theorem or perfect fingerprint is being asserted as a prerequisite; the remaining work is executable composite classification, reuse, and integration.

Complete-request mirror/conjugacy normalization, useful active behavioral transitions, comprehensive dependency coverage traces, and exact GC recreation attribution are still incomplete. Unknown attribution is not reported as zero or as proven recreation.
